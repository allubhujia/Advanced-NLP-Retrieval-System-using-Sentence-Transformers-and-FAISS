The PromptBuilder is a simple but essential utility that acts as the "Glue" between your retrieved data and the AI's response.

Its Role in RAG:
In a RAG system, you don't just send the user's question to the AI. You have to send the Context (the documents found in FAISS) along with it. The PromptBuilder formats this into a single string that the AI can understand.

Why use a separate class for this?
Consistency: It ensures that every question is wrapped in the same strict instructions (look at line 10: "Answer the question using only the provided context"). This is what makes it a "Grounded" RAG system.
Safety (Prevents Hallucination): It explicitly tells the AI to say "I don't know" if the answer isn't in your data. Without this, the AI might make up a fake answer based on its general training.
Clean Code: It keeps your main.py clean. Instead of having messy multi-line strings in your main logic, you just call prompt_builder.build_prompt(query, context).
